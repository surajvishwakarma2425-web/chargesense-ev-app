      Quick fixes

      The charger won’t start
      1. Check the plug is fully seated and the car is unlocked.
      2. Confirm your app or card is active and has balance.
      3. Unplug, wait 30 seconds, and try again.
      Charging is slower than expected
      1. Check the station’s rated power against your car’s limit.
      2. Speed drops above 80% charge.
      3. A cold battery charges slower.
      Range dropped suddenly
      1. Cold weather, air conditioning and high speed use more energy.
      2. Check tyre pressure.
      3. Use eco mode and plan the next charge earlier.
